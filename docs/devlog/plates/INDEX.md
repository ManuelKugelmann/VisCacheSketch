### `r3d_firefly_heatmap_{Sponza,BistroInterior,BistroExterior}.png`

Per-scene 3-up: R2d tonemap | R3d tonemap | err-diff heatmap
(magma colormap, R2d_err − R3d_err in OkLab). Bright regions in the
third panel = where R3d's cell-pool cleanup is operating.

Per-scene err-diff stats:

| Scene | max err-diff | mean err-diff | pattern |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Sponza | 1.449 | 0.288 | broad cleanup (high mean) |
| BistroInterior | 1.152 | 0.259 | broad cleanup |
| BistroExterior | 1.156 | 0.113 | localized hotspots (single bright sun) |

Use for: paper figure showing spatial distribution of the cleanup —
complements the brightness-stats compare plate by answering "where in
the frame is the cleanup happening?"

Source: `scripts/make_r3d_firefly_heatmap.py` (commit 7f0ff4a).

## Regenerate all plates

```sh
runtime/pythondist/python.exe scripts/make_r3d_firefly_compare.py
runtime/pythondist/python.exe scripts/make_r3d_firefly_heatmap.py
```

Reads existing captures from `runtime/captures/ladder/RPT_ZOO/*`. If the
RPT_ZOO ladder hasn't run (or was re-run with different SPP/bounce),
re-render first:

```sh
runtime/pythondist/python.exe scripts/run_ladder.py -s RPT_ZOO -c "Sponza,BistroInterior,BistroExterior"
```
