# 6. Eviction and Temporal Decay

Pressure-scaled eviction is always active on the insert path. Steps 0–2 are protected (no eviction at home slot or first two probes). From step 3, each step doubles the eviction threshold (8, 16, 32, 64, 128), enabling self-healing of long chains while giving fresh entries time to accumulate samples. Inline overflow decay uses a single InterlockedAdd to subtract 1/8 of both counters when total exceeds the active trigger threshold; default behaviour triggers at 0xE000 (~57K) to prevent 16-bit wrap-around, leaving the cell as a near-pure MLE running mean (effective forgetting window ≈ 50K samples). Integer shift-truncation preserves the mean ratio within ~0.003% at trigger counts. A known ABA race (read-then-subtract) can over-decay by ~1/waveSize ≈ 3% of decay events — negligible in practice.

**MLE α-floor.** When the trigger threshold is lowered to a target effective sample count $N^*$ (the `mlAlphaFloorN` knob, typ. 256–1024), the cell behaves as the discrete analogue of MCPG 2025 [Alber et al.]'s $\alpha = \max(1/N, \alpha_{\min})$ blending: while total < $N^*$ the cell is a pure running mean (every sample weighted equally, classical MLE), and once total ≥ $N^*$ the 1/8 decay fires per insert, holding total in $[7N^*/8, N^*]$ and giving each new sample a steady-state weight ≈ $1/N^*$. This converts the cell from "no forgetting until counters fill" to "explicit, tunable forgetting rate" — the rate controls how quickly the cell tracks scene changes without sacrificing the cold-start MLE behaviour. A single knob therefore selects between persistent (legacy, $N^* = $ off) and adaptive (small $N^*$) regimes; the existing 1/8 race-tolerant decay machinery is reused unchanged.

For dynamic scenes, an optional background decay pass traverses 1/DECAY_PERIOD of the table per frame, subtracting 1/8 of both counters on each visit (factor 0.875 per pass). An entry not refreshed decays geometrically: after K passes, counts scale as (7/8)^K. To reach 1/1024 of the original count requires K ≈ 52 passes. At DECAY_PERIOD=60 (~1 s at 60 fps): full decay in ~52 s. At DECAY_PERIOD=15: full decay in ~13 s. A PI controller auto-tunes DECAY_PERIOD based on eviction/insert ratio (target ~10%), speeding decay under load and slowing it in static scenes. Active entries resist decay because their sample rate (hundreds of inserts/frame at L0) far exceeds the decay rate (one 1/8 subtraction per DECAY_PERIOD frames). Not needed for single-frame rendering.
